Brief bio

I'm a researcher and engineer in blockchain.

I joined Babylon as a founding engineer in 2022. I helped drive the project through each pivot, from Bitcoin timestamping and staking to Trustless Bitcoin Vaults. My work spans protocol research, implementation, and launch.

Recently, I started advocating AI adoption and actively researching AI.

Previously, I obtained my PhD at Monash University and CSIRO-Data61 (advisors: Jiangshan Yu, Joseph Liu and Shiping Chen), Msc at The University of Manchester (advisor: Christos Kotselidis), and BSc at BUPT.

Research profile

Recently, I started researching how AI agents and formal verification can help invent new protocols for cryptography and distributed computing.

Engineering

FolderMy Computer / Engineering

Engineering profile

I build systems that support individual sovereignty and freedom. I enjoy taking research ideas through prototypes to production. I program mostly in Go and Rust.

I joined Babylon as a founding engineer in 2022 and worked through each pivot with the team. I helped take Bitcoin timestamping, staking, and chain integrations into production with Babylon Genesis. For Trustless Bitcoin Vaults, I contributed to the protocol design, built the first proof of concept, and helped deliver the testnet launch.

I advocate AI adoption and develop AI projects. My work includes reusable AI skills and workflows, open-source research tools, and AI agent workspaces.

[GitHub]

Selected repositories

  • BTC staking infrastructure. Babylon Genesis and Finality Provider bring Bitcoin security to proof-of-stake networks. Vigilante and BTC Staker support Bitcoin staking and communication between Babylon and Bitcoin.
  • BTC staking integration. Babylon SDK and contracts for Cosmos chains and rollups connect other chains to Bitcoin security.
  • Formal verification. Kriterion's argomac-lean provides a Lean implementation and security proofs for ArgoMAC.

Personal projects

  • AI research and reasoning. Reaper takes a research question through investigation to a written paper. Senate brings AI agents together for debate and collaborative decisions.
  • AI agent workspaces. Octopus lets developers direct AI coding agents on parallel tasks. ClawPier helps users manage isolated AI agent environments.

About this website

This site is my personal desktop on the web.

Windows XP provides the frame. The profile page and music player add personal details.

You control the desktop.

  • You can open a window with one click.
  • You can drag a window by its title bar.
  • You can minimize a window to the taskbar.
  • You can double-click a title bar to maximize a window.
  • You can use Tab and Enter to reach each control.
  • You can press Escape to close the Start menu.

The original site used mahbodnr's Windows-style homepage.

This site uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Webamp provides the music player.
