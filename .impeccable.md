## Design Context

### Users
Visitors explore a personal website and play with its desktop.

### Brand Personality
The site feels playful, personal, and nostalgic.

### Aesthetic Direction
The user chose a Windows XP desktop and a Google Gravity reference.
The controls use the existing XP colors, type, and window borders.

### Design Principles
- The desktop supplies the game objects.
- The controls stay available during play.
- The visitor can restore the desktop without a reload.
- The controls support a keyboard, mouse, and touch.
- Reduced motion starts the simulation paused.
